Questions

Do we need to buy any new software?

No. If your team has Microsoft 365 Business Standard or higher, everything runs on what you already have. We confirm this on the first call.

What if our process doesn’t fit a package?

Most do. If yours is bigger, we split it into packages or quote it separately, always as a fixed price. If you are not sure, the Process Check maps it for you.

Who has access to our systems?

We build and test in our own environment first. For installation you create a temporary account for us, and you remove it at handover.

What happens after handover?

You get a written guide and own everything. The optional care plan covers fixes and small changes.

How does payment work?

50% when you sign the one-page scope, 50% at handover. Two rounds of changes are included in every package.
