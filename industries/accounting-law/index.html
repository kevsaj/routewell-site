Questions from firms like yours

Is our client data safe?

Everything stays in your own Microsoft 365 tenant. We build and test with sample data in our environment, then install using a temporary account you create and remove at handover. We never copy client files out.

Does this replace our practice management software?

No. It handles the internal processes your practice software doesn’t: approvals, intake checklists and shared files. It can sit alongside it.

What Microsoft 365 plan do we need?

Business Standard or higher, which most firms already have. We check on the first call.

How long does it take?

Five to ten business days depending on the package, with a working demo before anything touches your live system.
