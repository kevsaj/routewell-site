Questions from firms like yours

Do crews need laptops?

No. Forms work in a phone browser or the Microsoft Teams app, and photos attach straight from the camera.

What if there’s no signal on site?

A standard form needs a connection to submit. If your crews often work out of range, we look at an offline-capable option on the first call.

What Microsoft 365 plan do we need?

Business Standard or higher for office staff. Field staff can often use lower-cost plans; we confirm on the first call.

Can it replace our job management software?

It’s meant for the gaps around it: forms, approvals and checklists that still run on paper. It can sit alongside what you use.
